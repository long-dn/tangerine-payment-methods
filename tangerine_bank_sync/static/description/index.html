Bank Information Vietnam

Keep the list of Vietnamese banks up to date from the VietQR directory: code, short name, NAPAS BIN, SWIFT code and logo. Find a bank by any of them and print VietQR codes that every banking app can read.

Features

VietQR directory synchronization
All Vietnamese banks and their code, short name, BIN, SWIFT code and logo are loaded at installation, then refreshed on demand or every day.
Search by code, short name, BIC or BIN
Type VCB, TCB or 970436 in any bank selector or in the Banks list: the bank is found even when its name does not contain it.
VietQR codes that banking apps can read
The NAPAS BIN of the bank is written into the VietQR payload of invoices. A bank account no longer needs a BIC to generate the QR code.
Banking menu
Banks and Bank Accounts are gathered in a Banking menu of the Invoicing app, available to billing users.

I. Bank directory

Install the module: the directory is downloaded from api.vietqr.io straight away. Open Invoicing > Banking > Banks to browse it.

Search a bank by its code, short name, SWIFT code or BIN.

To refresh the directory, use Actions > Sync from VietQR in the Banks list, or activate the scheduled action Bank Information Vietnam Synchronous (Settings > Technical > Scheduled Actions) to run it every day.

II. Bank accounts

In Invoicing > Banking > Bank Accounts (or on a contact), select the Bank by typing its code, short name or BIN.

III. VietQR on invoices

To print a VietQR code on customer invoices:

  • enable QR Codes in Invoicing > Configuration > Settings > Customer Payments;
  • fill the city of your company;
  • on your bank account, choose the Bank, set the Proxy Type to Bank Account and the Proxy Value to the account number.
Why the BIN matters

The standard Vietnamese localization writes the BIC of the bank where VietQR expects the NAPAS BIN. With a real SWIFT code there, banking apps cannot read the QR code. This module writes the BIN taken from the VietQR directory instead.
