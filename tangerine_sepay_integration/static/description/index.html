SePay Integration

Receive Vietnamese bank transfers through SePay, record the payments and reconcile the bank statement automatically. Print SePay VietQR codes on invoices and on the online payment pages.

Features

SePay VietQR codes
Dynamic QR codes with the amount due and the document reference, on invoices, on the customer portal and on the online payment pages.
Secure webhook
Every call is signed with HMAC-SHA256 and time-stamped. Secrets stay in odoo.conf and can differ per company. Replays are ignored.
Automatic payments and reconciliation
A transfer that designates exactly one invoice or order creates the payment, completes the online transaction and reconciles the bank statement line.
Review inbox
Every transfer is kept. Transfers that cannot be matched safely go to review with an activity; nothing is guessed.
API backfill
Optionally poll the SePay API to recover transfers whose webhook never arrived, without creating duplicates.
Virtual accounts
Encode the SePay virtual account in QR codes for banks that only report transfers made to a virtual account.

I. Prepare SePay

Discount Code

Use discount code EYVR1SYP to get 10% off the service package for the first registration.

Step 1: Register a SePay account here.

Step 2: Choose a service package and apply the discount code.

Step 3: Connect your bank account.

Step 4: Configure the payment code structure (Company configuration > General configuration) with the prefixes of your invoice and order numbers, e.g. INV for INV/2026/00001 and S for S00001. SePay then sends the reference in the code field; without it, the module looks the reference up in the transfer content.

Step 5: Create a webhook (Integrations > WebHooks) with:

EventIncoming money (or all transactions to review outgoing transfers too)
Bank accountThe account linked to your Odoo bank journal
Call URLhttps://<your Odoo domain>/webhook/sepay/v1/transactions
Payment verification webhookYes
Retry when the HTTP status is not 2xxYes
AuthenticationHMAC-SHA256 signature; keep the secret for odoo.conf
Request content typeapplication/json (form encodings are refused)

To recover missed webhooks, also create an API token (Company configuration > API Access).

II. Configure Odoo

Step 1: Add the secrets to the configuration file of the server and restart it. They are never stored in the database.

[options]
sepay_webhook_secret = <webhook HMAC secret>
sepay_api_token = <API token, optional>
; or per company, 1 being the company ID:
sepay_webhook_secret_1 = <secret of company 1>
sepay_api_token_1 = <token of company 1>

Step 2: In Invoicing > Configuration > Settings, enable SePay, choose the environment, the person who reviews exceptions, and keep Process Transactions Automatically on.

Step 3: Link your bank account to the bank journal (Configuration > Journals, bank journal) and make sure the journal uses VND and its Manual incoming payment method has an outstanding receipts account. On the bank account, choose the bank so the QR code knows it, and optionally the SePay virtual account.

Step 4: To take payments online, enable the Wire Transfer payment provider with the same bank journal.

Matching rules

Payments are created only when the transfer designates exactly one open invoice, or one sales order with a pending online payment. Transfers in other currencies, overpayments, outgoing transfers and ambiguous references are left for review on the bank journal, with their bank statement line.

III. QR codes

Customer invoices of a company using SePay select the SePay - VietQR payment QR code and the journal bank account by default.

The QR code carries the amount due and the payment reference.

It is also shown on the customer portal.

When a customer pays an invoice online with Wire Transfer or SePay, the pending payment shows the SePay QR code.

IV. Transactions

Open Invoicing > Banking > SePay Transactions. The default filter shows what needs action.

  • Received: stored, waiting for the scheduled action (it runs every minute).
  • Processed: the payment, the bank statement line and their reconciliation are done.
  • Needs Review: nothing was created except the bank statement line; reconcile it manually, then press Retry.
  • Error: retried automatically up to 5 times, then left to an accountant.
  • Ignored: closed on purpose (owner top-up, supplier refund, test transfer...).

V. Upgrading from version 1.x

  • Webhooks must now be signed: set the secret in odoo.conf and the HMAC authentication in SePay. The former URL /webhook/v1/payment/sepay keeps working.
  • SePay is enabled per company in the settings; companies located in Vietnam are enabled during the upgrade.
  • Transfers are stored first and processed by a scheduled action, which also reconciles the bank statement.
